**Why this topic:** "my bloodwork came back normal" comes up again and again on r/Perimenopause. NICE answers it clearly, and the answer leads straight to the product: a clear record of symptoms and cycles matters.

**Facts used (checked on nice.org.uk):**
- NICE NG23 (1.2): diagnose perimenopause without laboratory tests in otherwise healthy women over 45, based on vasomotor symptoms and irregular periods.
- NICE QS143: hormone levels fluctuate during perimenopause, so FSH tests don't help with the diagnosis. Other tests (e.g. thyroid function, blood count) may still be needed if other causes are suspected.
